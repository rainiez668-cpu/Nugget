# Real Leather. Stay Different. International Student Design Competition 2026

Source checked: 2026-06-15  
Official rules: https://rlsd.internationaldesigncomp.com/

## Participation

- Open worldwide.
- Entrants must be 18 or older.
- Open to currently enrolled students.
- Also open to people who graduated no more than one year ago and are not yet employed full-time in the creative industry.
- Entry is free.

## Categories

- Apparel
- Accessories
- Footwear

## Deliverables

- Submit at least 3 and no more than 5 images from different angles.
- One image must show a complete front view.
- Images must be landscape JPG files at 300 dpi.
- Each image must be no larger than 5 MB.
- Written description: maximum 600 words.

## Material and feasibility

- At least 50% of the design must use natural cattle leather.
- Fur and exotic skins are not allowed.
- Bonded leather and synthetic imitation leather cannot be used as the main material.
- The proposal must be capable of being produced as a physical prototype within approximately six months.

## AI

AI tools may be used in the design process, but the submitted design must remain original, feasible to manufacture, and compliant with all material requirements. Keep process evidence and follow any disclosure request from the organizer.
