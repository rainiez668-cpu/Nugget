# International Design Competition for Pohang Museum

Source checked: 2026-06-15  
Official page: http://www.pohang-muse.kr

## Participation verdict

This is a professional architectural competition. A student cannot independently qualify.

- Domestic entrants must hold the architectural qualifications required by Korean law.
- Foreign entrants must be legally qualified architects in their home country.
- A foreign winner must form a joint body with a Korean registered architect before entering the design-services contract.
- Qualification evidence must be submitted with registration.

## Schedule

- Registration deadline: 2026-07-20
- Submission deadline: 2026-09-07
- Entry fee: free

## Award

The winner receives the right to proceed with design services, subject to the official contract and qualification requirements.
